# Project ORBIT — Research Documentation

## Main Research Report

The main research report presents the investigation:

**Investigating Knowledge Retention and Adaptation in Q-Learning Agents Under Changing Environments**

It covers:

- Research background and objectives.
- Research questions.
- Simulation methodology.
- Experimental development and findings.
- Discussion and limitations.
- Conclusions and future research directions.

## Research Area

Artificial Intelligence and Reinforcement Learning.

## Research Approach

Computational simulation using tabular Q-learning agents in grid-based environments.

## Supporting Documentation

The technical appendix is available in the `technical/` directory. It contains the detailed methodology, experimental procedures, and Python source code.

## Research Scope

The investigation focuses on simulated navigation environments. Its findings are limited to the conditions tested and should not be treated as proof of performance in real-world robotics systems.
